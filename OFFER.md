# Visa Cohort Operations Sprint — Offer Brief

## One-sentence offer

For universities, associations, event teams and employers managing 20–50 visa-dependent travelers, the Visa Cohort Operations Sprint turns a privacy-minimized roster into one auditable readiness register within 10 business days, with every record labeled `READY`, `FIX REQUIRED` or `ESCALATE` and assigned an owner, deadline and next administrative action.

## Buyer

Operations, mobility, international-program, delegate-services, HR or travel leaders responsible for an organization-managed cohort with a real deadline.

## Disqualifiers

- Individual traveler cases
- Legal advice, eligibility analysis, category selection, appeals or representation
- Guaranteed approvals, appointments, processing speed, boarding or entry
- Requests to handle passwords, OTPs, payment cards or identity verification
- Cohorts without an authorized owner for legal/advisory escalations

## Single deliverable

One privacy-minimized **Cohort Readiness Register** covering up to 50 records.

Each record contains:

- Anonymous record reference
- Route/site
- Administrative status
- Status reason
- Next administrative action
- Responsible owner
- Deadline
- Evidence/source reference
- Escalation route when outside scope
- Last-updated timestamp

## Client inputs

1. Privacy-minimized roster and internal record references
2. Travel/program deadlines
3. Current status and known gaps
4. Official or adviser-approved requirement set
5. Named internal owners and authorized escalation contact

## Process

- Business days 1–2: source lock, roster normalization and boundary review
- Business days 3–7: record-by-record administrative readiness review
- Business days 8–9: exception, deadline and ownership QA
- Business day 10: authorized client review, one correction round and handover

## Price and payment

- Fixed price: **US$2,400**
- Scope: up to 50 records
- Payment: 50% at kickoff, 50% on delivery
- One correction round included
- Government, legal, translation, software, courier and third-party fees excluded

## Honesty boundary

Statuses describe administrative readiness only. They do not determine legal eligibility, predict government decisions, guarantee appointments or processing times, or replace counsel or official instructions. Customers retain control of accounts, identity verification, attestations, payment and final submission.

## Upgrade path

A separate ongoing operations engagement may be proposed only after the fixed sprint is delivered and the buyer confirms a recurring need. It is not part of the public offer.

## Fulfillment feasibility

The service is intentionally capped at 50 privacy-minimized records, one source set and one correction round. Out-of-scope questions are labeled `ESCALATE`, not improvised or answered as legal advice.
